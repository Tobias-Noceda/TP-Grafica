# Texture credits

All texture sets come from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) under the **CC0** license (public domain, no attribution
required). Files are the 1k JPG versions: diffuse (`diff`), OpenGL normal map (`nor_gl`) and ARM (`arm`: AO in R,
roughness in G, metalness in B).

| Folder              | Asset                                                                     | Author             | Real size |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | --------- |
| `concrete_tiles_02` | [Concrete Tiles 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_tiles_02)            | Charlotte Baglioni | 1.825 m   |
| `metal_plate_02`    | [Metal Plate 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate_02)                  | Rob Tuytel         | 2 m       |

The planet texture is not a file: it is generated at runtime in `src/app/space.js`.
